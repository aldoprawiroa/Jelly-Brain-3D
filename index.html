Inside the experiment

Anatomical SDF & Surface Nets (~1.7 mm): The outer cortex begins as a smooth union of six ellipsoids — two cerebral hemispheres, two temporal lobes, and a bilobed cerebellum with a flattened base. Gyri crowns are carved by the zero-sets of domain-warped 3D gradient noise alongside longitudinal, Sylvian, and transverse fissures and fine parallel cerebellar folia, polygonised once with Surface Nets.

BCC Co-Rotational XPBD & Cofactor Normals: A body-centered cubic (BCC) tetrahedral lattice is stuffed into the hull and snapped to the boundary. Running at 60 Hz with 8 substeps, each tetrahedron solves volume preservation and warm-started polar-decomposition shape matching. Surface vertices deform via barycentric weights while vertex normals ride the cofactor matrix (det(F)·F⁻ᵀ) averaged onto particles.

Six-Pass Beer–Lambert Optics: Six THREE.Scene passes compute a tinted PCSS-lite shadow map, top-down contact AO, an MSAA half-float interior pass (~30 firing nerve threads, bubbles, rigid candy eyes, planar lids, and flickering candle), back-face ray thickness, screen-space refraction with fold-weighted Beer–Lambert absorption and sulcus-dulled softbox reflections, and Khronos PBR Neutral tone mapping.
